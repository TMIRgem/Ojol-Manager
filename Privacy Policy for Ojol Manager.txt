Privacy Policy for Ojol Manager

Last updated: October 22, 2026

1. Overview
Ojol Manager ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you play our HTML5 Mini Game ("Ojol Manager") on TikTok.

2. Information Collection and Storage
- Local Device Storage: Ojol Manager operates locally within your web browser. All game progression, currency balances, upgrade levels, and game settings are saved strictly on your local device using your browser's `localStorage`.
- No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (PII), such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

3. Third-Party Services & TikTok Integration
Ojol Manager runs on the TikTok platform and may use the TikTok Developer SDK to display in-app advertisements (e.g., rewarded video ads) or handle user authentication. Third-party ad networks and TikTok may collect anonymous device identifiers or usage data in accordance with TikTok's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention and Deletion
Since all game data is stored locally on your device, you can clear or reset your game progress at any time by clearing your browser cache/data or clearing the app storage inside the TikTok app.

5. Children's Privacy
Ojol Manager does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game is intended for general audiences.
